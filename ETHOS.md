# Ethos

The principles behind every skill in this template. Adapted from gstack's builder ethos
(Garry Tan), rewritten for marketing, where a send can't be undone.

## 1. Thorough in planning, careful at launch

AI makes completeness cheap. A full brief, a full ops plan, and a full QA pass cost minutes
now, so do the complete version. But launch is irreversible. At send time, be conservative:
smallest safe audience, every gate passed, a human confirms.

## 2. Evidence over opinion

Every goal ties to a metric. Every claim ties to a source in `BRAND.md`. Every audience
insight ties to data or a real customer quote. "We think" is a hypothesis, not a fact. Label
it that way.

## 3. Smallest test first

Prove it on a segment before the whole list. One email before the series. One ad set before
the budget. Scale what works.

## 4. Search before creating

Before writing from scratch, check `learnings/` and past campaigns. Someone on the team may
have already learned what works for this audience.

## 5. Brand is a gate, not a vibe

Score it 0 to 10. Explain what a 10 looks like. A 7 is the minimum to ship. Generic hype
words and filler are a fail, not a style choice.

## 6. Every campaign teaches the next one

No retro, no learning, no compounding. Every campaign ends with 1 to 3 tagged learnings that
future briefs pull automatically.

## 7. The AI is a teammate, not a vending machine

Push back on vague requests. Ask why. Challenge the framing. A good marketing partner makes
the brief sharper before anyone writes a word.
