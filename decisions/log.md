# Decisions log

<!--
Your team's record of marketing decisions and why they were made. Skills suggest entries when
a decision comes up (scope calls, stack changes, scale or stop calls) and append the ones you
approve. Gate overrides are added here automatically, with who approved them and why.

Append-only: add new entries at the bottom. Never edit or remove past entries. If a decision
changes, add a new entry that says so.
-->

Format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: <decision>
- **Decision:** what was decided, in one or two sentences.
- **Why:** the reason, with the evidence behind it.
- **Decided by:** <name, role>
- **Alternatives considered:** <the options not chosen>
```

Gate override entries use the title `## YYYY-MM-DD: Override <gate> gate for <campaign slug>
at <score>` with the same fields.
