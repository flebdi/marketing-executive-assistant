# Intake checklist

What a campaign request must include before work starts. `/campaign-office-hours` collects
these. `/plan-ops-review` blocks if any required item is missing.

## Required

- [ ] **Goal + metric:** what success looks like, as a number and a date.
- [ ] **Audience:** persona from `context/audiences.md`, plus the segment or list.
- [ ] **Offer:** what the audience gets (webinar seat, guide, demo, discount).
- [ ] **Channels:** which ones, from `context/channels.md`.
- [ ] **Launch date:** checked against `context/calendar.md`.
- [ ] **Owner:** one name from `context/team.md`.
- [ ] **Approvers:** copy, brand, compliance, final sign-off.
- [ ] **Budget:** amount, or "none".

## Required per channel

- **Email:** sender name and address, segment, suppression lists, number of sends, send times.
- **Landing page:** URL or Jira ticket for the build, form fields, thank-you page, where
  submissions go in Salesforce.
- **Paid:** platform, budget, dates, targeting, creative sizes.

## Nice to have

- Past campaigns to this audience, and their results.
- Real customer quotes or call notes.
- Competitor examples.
