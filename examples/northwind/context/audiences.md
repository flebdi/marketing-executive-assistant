## ICP (ideal customer profile)

- **Company size:** 200-2,000 employees
- **Industry:** B2B SaaS
- **Region:** North America (US, Canada)
- **Buying trigger:** Close takes more than a week, or a new CFO / board asks for faster reporting

## Persona: VP Finance

- **Title(s):** VP Finance, Head of Finance, Controller (larger accounts)
- **Reports to:** CFO
- **What gets them promoted:** A faster, cleaner close; numbers the board trusts
- **What gets them fired:** A missed or restated board number
- **Top 3 pains:** Close drags past day 7; reconciliations live in spreadsheets; last-minute adjustments before board meetings
